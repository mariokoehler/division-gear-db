Data notes

All 64 Brand Sets and Gear Sets below were extracted directly from the game's own data files (Snowdrop engine .sdftoc/.sdfdata archives, via the community tool Hunter), then cross-checked against wiki data. This resolved every brand/gear set that was previously missing from community sources, caught a few brands whose bonus values had changed in a balance patch since the wiki was last updated, and (with an in-game assist for two final details — Unit Alloys' 1-piece stat and Refactor's 4-piece talent tooltip) reached full coverage with no known gaps.
